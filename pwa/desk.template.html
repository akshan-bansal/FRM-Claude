Influence is a second, dimensionless channel, separate from weight and clamped to (0, 1] — intelligence may de-risk and can never lever up. Venue is capped at 0.65: a venue records where a trade cleared, not why it happened. The bars are each type’s share of total influence, so raising one necessarily lowers the rest rather than inflating the total.

Thickness is edge count, never summed weight. weight is not one quantity: for traded it is notional dollars, for observed a decayed observation score, and ranked_by carries a signed score that sums negative. Pooling them would draw a picture whose thickest line means dollars and whose thinnest means score. Per-predicate sums are listed below with their own units and are not compared.

The IB futures book needs its own TWS or IB Gateway login with a listening port. This tab probes the ports at the moment the shim answers, so it is only drawn from a running session's /desk. A listening port proves something is there, not that it is the right account, has market-data permissions, or has the API enabled.

WHAT THE ATLAS DOES NOT CLAIM

The catalogue states what the code defines, not what has been observed: a meter with no measurement is drawn neutral and without a needle, and no value is substituted for it. Bounded meters print their endpoints; unbounded quantities show numbers only; identity and lifecycle meters are status tiles, not percentages. A snapshot is a file, not a streaming connection — it is parsed in this page, nothing is uploaded, no broker is contacted, and a reading without a source, a scope and a status is rejected along with the whole file. Click any tile for its definition, unit, scale, code support and pinned source. The $150 hardware tier is a proposed target and says nothing about the status of any meter here.
